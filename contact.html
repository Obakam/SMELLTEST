    Questions

    Before you visit

    How long does a fragrance last on skin?

    Our eau de parfum sits at 18–22% concentration and lasts six to eight hours; the extraits at 30–32% run ten to twelve. Longevity always depends on skin, heat and humidity — which is why we offer a consultation before you commit to a full bottle.

    Can I try before buying a full bottle?

    Yes. The Discovery Set contains five 2 ml vials of the signature collection, and the cost is credited against your first full bottle. Single samples are also available at the atelier.

    Can I cancel or move an appointment?

    Yes, with 48 hours' notice at no cost. Later cancellations are charged a small studio fee, which is credited against a future purchase.

    Do you ship outside Lagos?

    We deliver nationwide within Nigeria and ship internationally by DHL. Delivery is complimentary within Lagos on orders above ₦150,000. Fragrances travel by road within Nigeria, as air freight restrictions apply to alcohol-based liquids.

    How should I store my bottle?

    Keep it upright, away from direct sunlight and heat, with the cap on. A wardrobe shelf is better than a bathroom ledge. Return any 100 ml bottle to the atelier and we will refill it at 30% off.
