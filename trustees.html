Board of Trustees

Five members, five portfolios. The board meets quarterly to safeguard the house's craft standards, its people and its finances.

Stewardship

The board

Collective responsibility for craft, people and place.

Margaret Ellison

Chairperson

Sets the direction of the house and chairs the quarterly board. Twenty-two years in luxury retail across London and Lagos.

Portfolio: Governance, house strategy and the annual collection calendar.

Daniel Whitmore

Vice Chairperson

Deputises for the chair and leads the atelier's technical standards, from pattern-cutting to final pressing.

Portfolio: Production, quality control and the apprenticeship programme.

Charlotte Hayes

Company Secretary

Keeps the minutes, the register and this website. Reads the fine print so that nobody else has to.

Portfolio: Documentation, compliance and the digital presence of the house.

James Ashcroft

Treasurer

Owns the numbers: budgets, supplier contracts and the wages paid to every artisan in the atelier.

Portfolio: Costing, procurement and fair-trade sourcing of raw materials.

Eleanor Bennett

Marketing & Brand Director

Runs the launches, the press and the mailing list — and reads every customer letter that arrives at the studio.

Portfolio: Campaigns, events, partnerships and customer care.
