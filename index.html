Our house

Composed slowly, in small batches

SMELLTEST began in 2019 with three tailors, one loom and a borrowed cutting table. The house still cuts cloth by hand — and since 2024 it has also composed fragrance, in a small lab above the atelier.

  • 01

    Ingredients traced to source

    Frankincense from Plateau State, vetiver from Abeokuta, ylang-ylang from the Comoros. Nothing synthetic at the top of the pyramid.

  • 02

    Composed on skin

    Every formula is tested for six weeks on real skin, in Lagos humidity, before a single bottle is filled.

  • 03

    Refilled, not replaced

    Return any 100 ml bottle to the atelier and we refill it at 30% off, for the life of the formula.
